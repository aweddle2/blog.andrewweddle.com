I've always wondered if I could.  YOu knwo you see those videos from Bali about the digial nomads working on a laptop in a cafe.

## Screen real estate
I first got a dual screen setup somewhere between 2000 and 2002.  2 x 17" CRTs.  they were massive and took up nearly the whole desk.  But I loved and I've never worked on a single screen since.

The thought of going "nomadic" and having to work from just a single laptop screen was a show stopper.  No way, never again.

Fast forward 20 years and here I am sitting with my fairly compact dual screen setup writing this blog.
